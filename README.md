# ✈️ Ensemble Learning for Airline Customer Satisfaction

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed by **Nowa Analytics** as part of a consultancy case study in **Ensemble Learning and Classification** applied to the **airline industry**.
The objective is to combine multiple classifiers to **predict customer satisfaction with flights**, exploring techniques such as **Voting, Bagging, Boosting, and Stacking** to maximize predictive performance.

Since the dataset is initially unknown, the project includes **data exploration**, **model selection**, and the evaluation of ensemble methods to achieve the most reliable results.

---

## 🎯 Business Context

Airline companies collect extensive data about their flights and passenger experiences, such as:

* Flight details (duration, departure/arrival delays, class, seat comfort, etc.)
* Passenger demographics
* Service ratings (food, entertainment, cleanliness, staff service, etc.)
* Overall satisfaction feedback

The business problem is to predict the **customer satisfaction level**:

* `0` → Dissatisfied passenger
* `1` → Satisfied passenger

By identifying dissatisfied passengers in advance, the airline can:

* Implement service improvements,
* Personalize communication,
* Reduce customer churn,
* Enhance overall loyalty.

---

## 🧠 Project Objectives

* Perform **data exploration** to understand customer behavior and flight characteristics.
* Train and evaluate **base classifiers** (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forests, Gradient Boosting, etc.).
* Apply **Voting Classifiers** for more reliable predictions.
* Use **Bagging** to increase model robustness and reduce variance.
* Apply **Boosting techniques** (e.g., AdaBoost, XGBoost, LightGBM) to optimize accuracy.
* Integrate models with **Stacking** to achieve cutting-edge results in AI classification.
* Compare model performances and select the most effective ensemble approach.
